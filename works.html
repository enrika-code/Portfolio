This small collection of experimental web pages is the result of work I started during my school years and continued through independent study, with the aim of putting what I have learned into practice. I have consciously left my early projects in my portfolio, as they show the evolution of my skills and I am proud of them. Although I am fully aware that I still have much to learn, I believe perfection comes with experience. I am deeply inspired by nature, harmony, and simplicity, and I try to bring the same clean approach to both my code and my design. For me, front-end development is a way to express myself. I love beauty, always put real care into my work, and constantly seek new challenges that allow me to grow. I have a strong desire for personal and professional growth, and I would like to reach my full potential and take my work to the next level by working within a team and contributing to its success.
